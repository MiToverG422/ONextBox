Root allows ONextBox to execute commands with the highest device privilege. LSPosed loads module code inside Android, System UI, Settings, the launcher, and other selected processes.

Incorrect settings, incompatible system updates, or conflicts with other modules can weaken security or cause instability. Only grant access on a device you are authorized to manage. Review each scope in LSPosed and keep a tested way to disable modules or enter recovery.

Custom display density changes the scale of the whole interface. Turning it off restores the previous density. If you choose to keep it after restart, ONextBox reapplies it at boot. Glass rendering and camera modes can vary by system version and device hardware.
